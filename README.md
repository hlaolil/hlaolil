# Hi, I'm Letlotlo Hlaoli 👋

**Pharmacist · Minister · Developer** — based in Lesotho 🇱🇸

I build software for the places I actually work: pharmacies, clinics, and NGOs in Lesotho. Most of what's here started as a paper register or a spreadsheet that someone was tired of filling in by hand.

By day I'm a pharmacist and study software development on the side. The code is where those worlds meet.

## 🏥 Health & pharmacy systems

### [NMC Pharmacy — HealthRecords](https://github.com/hlaolil/HealthRecords)
The day-to-day pharmacy system for a workplace clinic. Dispensing, receiving stock, stock takes, expiry management, department requests, equipment loans, a key register, fridge temperature logs, reports, an audit trail and user accounts with roles.
`Python` `Flask` `MongoDB` `Gunicorn` · 70+ tests

### [The Healing Hub Pharmacy — Stock & Point of Sale](https://github.com/hlaolil/healing-hub-stock)
Adapted from HealthRecords for my own retail pharmacy. Adds a till (cash, M-Pesa, EcoCash, card, medical aid), receipts, daily cash-up, gross profit, the controlled drug register, and a product feed that powers the public website.
`Python` `Flask` `MongoDB` `Vercel`

### [The Healing Hub Pharmacy — Website](https://github.com/hlaolil/healing-hub-pharmacy)
The pharmacy's public site in Ha Leqele, Maseru. Patients can request refills online and sign up for email refill reminders, and the "What We Stock" page shows live prices and stock from the till.
`Next.js 14` `React` `Postgres` `Nodemailer`

### [NMC Records — Clinic HSE app](https://github.com/hlaolil/nmc-records)
One web app replacing the clinic's health-and-safety spreadsheets: daily emergency trolley and equipment checks, equipment register and budget, a hazardous substances register with MSDS review dates, and the baseline risk assessment.
`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Neon Postgres`

### [Grace Pharmacy — Website](https://github.com/hlaolil/gracepharmacy)
A community pharmacy site for Morija, with a refill form, SEO basics and a visual identity built from the pharmacy's own flyer and logo.
`Next.js 14` `React` `Postgres`
